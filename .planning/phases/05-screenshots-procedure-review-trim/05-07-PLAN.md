<objective>
Make the captured screenshots visible to the doctor while the procedure is still in progress (mid-procedure gallery in ProcedureRoom), make the per-thumbnail delete affordance genuinely discoverable in a clinical setting, and add a full-size lightbox so the doctor can verify clinical detail at the captured JPEG's native 1280-px resolution. Three gaps, one component surface — same IPC paths, same ScreenshotTimeline reuse, same /media/ route, no new server work.

Purpose: G-05-8/9/10 are mid-severity but together they leave the screenshot feature half-finished — the doctor captures frames but cannot easily see them, cannot easily delete them, and cannot easily inspect them at clinical detail. The Phase 5 promise was "capture during OR after a procedure"; the mid-procedure side is currently invisible.

Output: ScreenshotTimeline mounted in ProcedureRoom's right aside fed by the existing hook state; × button is a 24×24 solid-red badge; new ScreenshotLightbox modal mounted in ProcedureReview (and reachable from the gallery via the same onOpen prop); tests assert the gallery mount, the × class list, the expand affordance, and the lightbox DOM.
</objective>
